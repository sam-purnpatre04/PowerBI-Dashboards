# 📊 Power BI Dashboard Portfolio

Welcome to my **Power BI Dashboard Portfolio**! This repository showcases interactive business intelligence dashboards built using real-world datasets. Each project focuses on transforming raw data into meaningful insights through **data cleaning, data modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive visualizations**.

The objective of these dashboards is to support **data-driven decision-making** by uncovering trends, patterns, performance gaps, and key business metrics.

---

## 🚀 Projects

### 📈 Marketing Campaign Analysis Dashboard

This dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of **marketing campaign performance**, helping identify successful campaigns, optimize marketing spending, and understand customer conversion behavior.

**Key Insights**

* Campaign Revenue Analysis
* Marketing Spend Analysis
* Return on Investment (ROI)
* Conversion Performance
* Regional Performance Comparison
* Customer & Campaign Trends
* Marketing KPI Summary Dashboard

---

### 👥 HR Analytics Dashboard

This dashboard analyzes **workforce and employee data** to understand attrition patterns, workforce demographics, employee satisfaction, and organizational trends.

**Key Insights**

* Employee Attrition Analysis
* Department-wise Workforce Distribution
* Gender Diversity Analysis
* Age Group & Experience Analysis
* Job Role Analysis
* Employee Satisfaction Trends
* Retention & Turnover Metrics

---

### 🚚 Supply Chain & Performance Analysis Dashboard

This dashboard analyzes **supply chain and delivery operations** to identify bottlenecks, evaluate supplier and shipping performance, and improve overall operational efficiency.

**Key Insights**

* Revenue & Product Performance Analysis
* Order Fulfillment & On-Time Delivery Rates
* Delivery Delay & Lead Time Analysis
* Supplier Performance & Reliability
* Shipping Mode & Route Efficiency Analysis
* Regional & Warehouse Performance
* Return Rate & Customer Rating Analysis
* Supply Chain KPI Summary Dashboard
